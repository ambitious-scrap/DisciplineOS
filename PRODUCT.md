# PRODUCT.md — DisciplineOS

## Product Vision
**DisciplineOS** is an authoritative, cross-device attention defense and focus operating system designed for high-performance builders, engineers, and athletes. It eliminates digital distractions across mobile (Android) and desktop (macOS & Web) through non-negotiable enforcement, cryptographic time banking, and real-world proof-of-work verification.

## Target Audience
- Founders, software engineers, and knowledge workers battling algorithmic feeds and infinite scroll.
- High-agency individuals seeking unbending self-discipline and sovereign control over their time.

## Core Pillars & Mechanics
1. **Authoritative Lock & Single Distraction Session:** Only one distracting app or domain can be unlocked globally at any given moment.
2. **Immutable Time Bank:** Focus minutes must be earned through verified physical habits (e.g. gym workout with SHA-256 live camera proof) or deep focus sprints.
3. **Emergency Unlock Penalty:** Emergency overrides deduct a strict 3x cost penalty from future reserves.
4. **Multi-Surface Enforcement:** Android UsageStats + DNS VPN + macOS Menu Bar agent + Chrome Extension.

## Tone and Voice
- **Tone:** Sovereign, tactical, resolute, precise, and empowering.
- **Visual Stance:** Cyber-hardware precision, OLED tactile depth, high contrast, zero fluff.
